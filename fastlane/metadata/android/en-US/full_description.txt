MuslimVN is an open-source mobile app designed for the Muslim community in Vietnam and worldwide, providing a comprehensive, accurate, ad-free, and privacy-focused Islamic companion.

✨ KEY FEATURES:

🕌 ACCURATE OFFLINE PRAYER TIMES & ADHAN NOTIFICATIONS
• Precise prayer times calculated using the Adhan astronomical algorithm tailored for locations in Vietnam.
• Works 100% offline without needing an active internet connection.
• Custom Adhan audio reminders with reciters (Mishary Alafasi, Hamad Daghriry, etc.).
• Choose your city from 63 provinces/cities in Vietnam or use automatic location lookup.

🧭 PRECISE QIBLA FINDER
• Real-time compass navigation using device sensors and geographic coordinates to point accurately toward the Kaaba (Makkah).

📖 HOLY QURAN WITH VIETNAMESE TRANSLATION
• Read all 114 Surahs with high-quality Arabic text and clean Vietnamese translations.
• Listen to audio recitations per Ayah or Surah from renowned Qaris.
• Bookmarks, Surah search, and flexible layout options.

📅 HIJRI CALENDAR & ISLAMIC EVENTS
• Lunar Hijri calendar with manual date adjustment support for local moon sighting.
• Track key Islamic dates and holidays (Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, etc.).

🧮 FIQH-COMPLIANT ZAKAT CALCULATOR
• Comprehensive calculator for Zakat on Cash, Gold, Silver, Stocks, Real Estate, and Agriculture.

🤲 DAILY AZKAR & 99 NAMES OF ALLAH
• Morning/Evening and post-prayer supplications from Hisnul Muslim.
• Read and learn the 99 Beautiful Names of Allah with meanings.

🎙️ SCHOLARS & PODCASTS
• Stream world-class Islamic lectures and podcasts from Muslim Central.
• Localized content from trusted Vietnamese Muslim scholars.

🔒 PRIVACY & OPEN SOURCE
• 100% Open Source (GPLv3), zero personal data tracking, no analytics, no ads.
